# AI Social Media Manager Setup

## Description

Set up a human-approved AI social media workflow that uses Love Affair's existing content, brand voice, and media library to create approval-ready social posts and content calendars.

## Short Service Name Options

- AI Social Media Manager Setup
- AI-Assisted Social Media Operations System
- Human-Approved AI Social Media Workflow
- AI Content Calendar and Scheduling System
- Brand-Trained Social Media Assistant

## What They Are Looking For

Love Affair wants an AI-assisted social media manager that can work from their existing brand materials instead of inventing unrelated AI-generated content.

The system should help with:

- Understanding and applying Love Affair's brand voice
- Reviewing existing content from Google Drive
- Finding suitable images or videos from the existing content library
- Creating a social media content calendar
- Writing captions and hashtags
- Preparing posts in Later or a similar scheduling platform
- Keeping humans in the approval loop before anything goes live

## Proposed Service Description

I would set up an AI-assisted social media workflow for Love Affair that turns their existing brand content into scheduled, approval-ready social posts.

The system would use their brand voice, Google Drive content library, and preferred scheduling tool to generate post ideas, select matching images, write captions and hashtags, and organize everything into a content calendar. Love Affair would still review, approve, adjust, or reject posts before publishing.

## Important Boundary

This should not be positioned as "fully autonomous social media management."

The safer and more accurate positioning is:

> AI prepares the social media calendar and post drafts. Humans approve before publishing.

## Likely Workflow

1. Define Love Affair's social media goals, channels, audience, and posting cadence.
2. Capture brand voice examples from existing posts, website copy, menus, event language, and founder input.
3. Organize the existing Google Drive content library so the AI can understand what media is available.
4. Create prompt templates or an assistant workflow for post planning, image selection, captions, and hashtags.
5. Connect the workflow to a scheduling tool such as Later, Buffer, Metricool, or another platform if practical.
6. Build an approval process so posts are reviewed before publishing.
7. Train Hilton and Viv on how to run, adjust, and maintain the system.

## Deliverables

- Brand voice profile
- Content library structure
- AI prompt/workflow templates
- Content calendar generation process
- Caption and hashtag generation process
- Scheduling platform setup or recommendation
- Human approval workflow
- Training session and basic operating guide

## Discovery Questions

- Which social channels matter most right now?
- How often do they want to post?
- What types of posts perform best for Love Affair?
- Where is their strongest existing content stored?
- Is the Google Drive already organized by events, dates, campaigns, or content type?
- Do they want the system to draft only, or also upload/schedule into a platform?
- Which scheduling platform do they prefer if Later is not the right fit?
- Who gives final approval before posts go live?

## One-Line Pitch

An AI-assisted social media manager setup that turns Love Affair's existing content library and brand voice into approval-ready calendars, captions, hashtags, and scheduled post drafts.
