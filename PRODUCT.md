## Platform

web

## Users

Equal Sons serves small and medium-sized businesses that are ready to move from an idea, ambiguity, or stalled initiative into execution. Buyers need a hands-on partner for product development, strategic direction, communication, or a combination of the three.

## Product Purpose

Equal Sons is a boutique consulting firm based in Richmond, Virginia. It partners with businesses to build digital products, lead strategy, and share ideas that create real momentum. Success means turning a client's intent into tangible products, clearer direction, stronger communication, or measurable business progress.

## Positioning

Equal Sons combines product development, strategic leadership, and executive communication in one hands-on team. The firm works alongside clients through execution instead of stopping at recommendations or handing off a strategy deck.

## Operating Context

Engagements span custom web and app development, MVP creation, systems integration, technology and marketing strategy, product positioning, facilitation, mediation, public speaking, workshops, and training. The three service pillars are **Build**, **Lead**, and **Share**; they may stand alone or work together.

The public website explains these capabilities, presents case studies and team expertise, and converts interest through direct outreach and a protected contact form.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the **Build. Lead. Share.** service model and terminology.
- Present Equal Sons as a collaborative partner that connects strategy to execution.
- Do not invent customers, testimonials, performance metrics, credentials, or business outcomes.
- Treat accessibility as a requirement in all implementation work.
- The website is a web product. The current implementation is a React and Vite frontend with a Cloudflare Workers form API; repository architecture may evolve without changing the product definition.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Equal Sons**
- Master tagline: **Build. Lead. Share.**
- Core positioning: a boutique consulting firm for businesses ready to move
- Location: Richmond, Virginia, working with clients beyond the region
- Voice: confident but not arrogant; warm but not casual; active rather than passive; clear rather than clever
- Prefer direct, concrete language such as build, create, partner, momentum, clarity, and direction.
- Avoid corporate jargon, vague consulting language, hype, and unsupported superlatives.
- The authoritative brand foundation and approved website copy are in `docs/brand-strategy.md`.

## Product Principles

1. Turn ideas and strategy into concrete execution.
2. Work alongside clients as an embedded partner, not a distant advisor.
3. Create clarity and momentum without unnecessary complexity.
4. Connect technical, strategic, and communication work when the problem crosses disciplines.
5. Let real work and evidence carry the message.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Design and implementation must account for accessibility from the outset, including semantic structure, keyboard operation, readable contrast, clear focus states, meaningful alternatives for non-text content, and reduced-motion preferences where motion is used.
